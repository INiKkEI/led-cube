# LED Cube — Risk Register

Likelihood and impact are High, Medium or Low. Impact is judged against the requirements in `requirements.md`.

| ID | Risk | Lik. | Imp. | Mitigation |
|---|---|:--:|:--:|---|
| RISK-01 | Visible brightness steps every eight columns. Each shift register's output resistance differs, and all eight of its columns inherit it. Fails SPEC-12. | M | M | Buy all nine registers from one reel or batch. Measure each chip's output voltage under load before fitting. The 220 Ω resistor is already sized to dominate the variation. |
| RISK-02 | Blue LED forward voltage higher than the assumed 3.2 V. **Confirmed against datasheets** — every candidate blue LED specifies 3.2–4.0 V, none specifies a flat 3.2 V. At 3.7 V the current falls to about 4.5 mA, 29 % below the design point; at 4.0 V it falls to 3.5 mA. | H | H | Measure Vf at 6 mA on ten delivered LEDs, then choose the resistor from that measurement. ADR-05 is held provisional until this is done. |
| RISK-03 | Shift registers run at 47–53 mA against a 70 mA absolute maximum. Absolute maximum ratings are limits, not design points. | L | H | Never fit a resistor below 220 Ω. Confirm the per-package current with ST-12 and ST-19 before any long run. |
| RISK-04 | ESP32 interrupt jitter breaks the refresh timing. Interrupt handlers left in flash are blocked whenever flash is accessed, and the WiFi stack pre-empts freely. Fails SPEC-07 and SPEC-08. | M | H | Put the refresh handler in IRAM, leave WiFi and Bluetooth disabled, pin the timer to one core, and keep flash reads out of the interrupt path. |
| RISK-05 | Ghosting — faint light on LEDs that should be off. With OE grounded there is no blanking, so the only protection is that columns and layer update on the same latch edge. The residual exposure is MOSFET switching overlap: for roughly 2 µs the new pattern sits on the layer that is turning off, about 0.3 % of a layer period. Fails SPEC-10. | M | M | Keep the layer byte in the same 72-bit word as the columns so a single latch updates both — never update them separately. If ghosting is visible, blank in software by latching all-zero column bytes before the real frame. |
| RISK-06 | 5 mm LED leads too short for the 900 mil pitch. Typical leads are 24–26 mm; bending and joining consumes length, and the pitch is 22.86 mm. | M | H | Measure the leads on the LEDs you actually buy before building the jig. If they are short, run tinned copper wire for the horizontal rows instead of relying on the leads. |
| RISK-07 | Lattice too floppy to stand or be carried. 160 mm of unsupported lead. Fails SPEC-29 or SPEC-30. | M | M | Use solid wire for the horizontal rows rather than lead-to-lead joints. Build and test one full layer before committing to all eight. |
| RISK-08 | First PCB revision has errors and needs a respin. This is the first board designed in Altium. | H | M | Run ERC and DRC, have the schematic reviewed, and breadboard one shift register with one MOSFET and eight LEDs before ordering. Order only one or two boards on the first run. |
| RISK-09 | Layer register outputs overstressed on every switch. With gates driven directly, the peak is about 75 mA against a 35 mA per-pin absolute maximum — brief (~2 µs) but 1600 times a second. Damage would appear as a layer that stops switching, or a register that fails after weeks rather than immediately. | M | H | Fit a 470 Ω series resistor in each gate line if it becomes a problem; the footprints cost nothing to include now and can be shorted with a wire link if unused. Watch for a dead or dim layer during the 4 h run in ST-21. |
| RISK-10 | No local decoupling. A single 470 µF electrolytic at the jack is the only capacitor; the nine registers have nothing at their supply pins. An electrolytic has too much inductance to respond to nanosecond switching edges, so the 5 V rail will show local spikes as 72 outputs change state 1600 times a second. Shows up as occasional wrong LEDs or corrupt frames, which looks like a firmware bug. | M | H | Put a 100 nF footprint next to each register's pins 16 and 8 and leave it unfitted. If glitching appears, fit nine ceramics — a few cents and no board change. Watch for stray lit LEDs during IT-14. |
| RISK-11 | LEDs bought in bulk without a datasheet (ADR-18) arrive unbinned. Intensity and Vf spread across 512 pieces is unknown and could break SPEC-12's ±20 %. There is no published figure to check before they arrive. | M | M | Order 1000 and use the best 512. On arrival, light twenty at once and reject visible outliers before any are soldered. Measure Vf across ten to bound the spread. Watch during IT-13, which is the first test that lights a full layer. |
